Terms and privacy notice

NordBank is a fictional educational banking simulation. It does not provide real banking services, hold real funds, issue payment instruments, or process real payments.

Information you provide

Your name, email address, optional phone number, and account activity are stored in this project’s database so the application can demonstrate customer workflows. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Using the simulation

Do not enter real financial account information, payment card data, passwords used on other services, or other sensitive personal data. Simulated transfers only affect records in this application.
